# Relay Owner Quick Start

## 1. Forward missed calls

Keep your existing business number. Enable **conditional call forwarding** so
Relay receives calls only when you do not answer. Follow the carrier-specific
steps on Relay's Setup page, then place one real test call and let it ring
unanswered. The call should appear once in the Relay inbox.

## 2. Work the inbox

Open **Leads** for each missed call. Listen to the voicemail, review its
transcript and summary, add a caller name or note when useful, and move the lead
from **New** to **Contacted** or **Closed** as the conversation progresses.

## 3. Reply to the caller

Reply from the lead conversation so the message comes from your Relay number
and stays with the lead. Use a quick reply when it fits, but personalize the
answer. Automatic missed-call texts remain off until carrier approval is
complete and you choose to enable them.

## 4. Record bookings

When a lead becomes work, select **Mark as booked** and enter the expected or
final job value. Booking is an outcome, so the lead can remain Contacted or
Closed while still counting as booked.

## 5. See recovered value

Open **Reports** to review missed calls, replies, response time, bookings, and
the total value entered on booked leads. Add a value to every booked job;
otherwise Relay will show the booking but cannot count its recovered dollars.

## Help Relay verify launch

Confirm that you received the owner-notification test on the Setup page. After
the real call, voicemail, SMS delivery, reply, and booking checks all pass,
approve go-live from your authenticated account.
